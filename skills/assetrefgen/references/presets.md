# Quick and Detailed

The preset controls inspection and correction effort, not identity, style, subject count, required views or provider choice. The user does not need to write the orchestration prompt.

| Behavior | Quick (default; Quick Prototype) | Detailed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity brief | Concise silhouette, anatomy/parts, colors, distinctive features, pose/axes and provisional hidden details | Fuller feature/side correspondence table, landmarks, material boundaries and construction notes |
| Every output | Inspect intended subject, complete framing, major anatomy/components, approximate direction and recognizable identity; validate file | Also inspect seams, attachments, surface continuity, asymmetry, scale/framing landmarks and incidentals across the set |
| Minor drift | Record and accept usable variation | Attempt a bounded targeted improvement when useful |
| Automatic correction budget | At most **one targeted correction per asset total**, including master and views | At most **three targeted corrections per asset total** |
| Evidence | Exact requests, raw output, ordered reference hashes, selections, concise QA and all attempted corrections | Same core evidence plus fuller per-view findings and before/after comparisons |
| Finish | Usable temporary modeling references with known caveats | More thoroughly reviewed references with known caveats |

Always preserve an existing satisfactory master. Optional detail/spread-wing/top views follow the four required directions for the roster unless the user prioritizes otherwise. Neither preset guarantees mathematical orthographic consistency or validates meshes, rigging, anatomy clearances or topology.

Record correction budget use **before** calling the service so interruption cannot reset it. Failed service calls are separate from visual corrections: allow at most one retry of a transient failure after unaffected work, honoring service retry timing. Repeated transient errors stop that task. Never automatically retry a policy rejection or rephrase it to evade restrictions. A rejection affecting one subject does not stop unrelated subjects.

Each reservation permits one corrective generation call. Settle it with the selected/rejected result even if the image stayed unchanged, the service returned no image, or the rest of the asset is still incomplete. Additional corrective calls require additional budget; an open correction is not a reusable allowance. Preserve known-good views when an individual direction is rejected and exclude the unusable direction from selected review deliverables.

If the major-error budget is exhausted, preserve the best usable candidate, note its caveats, and mark any unusable direction incomplete. Do not label a wrong identity or missing major limb acceptable merely to satisfy a completion counter. Quick is not an instruction to skip actual visual inspection.

A QA pass is an agent's visual judgment for the chosen purpose. It is not the user's final artistic acceptance. Keep both approval flags false unless the user has actually granted the corresponding approval.
