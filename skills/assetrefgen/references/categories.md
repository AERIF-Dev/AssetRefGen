# Category rules

Apply the relevant category to the shared workflow. Preserve the user's style and four requested views; add essential extras without replacing requested views.

## Coordinates
Define local forward/up first. Front looks at the front, with asset-local left on image-right. Left elevation shows that side with forward pointing image-left; right points image-right; back faces away. Do not use viewer-relative side naming. Document alternative user conventions and handedness. In a right-handed example with forward +Y and up +Z, local left is -X; do not assign incompatible signed axes. For oblique source objects, determine which face is visible and how it would turn upright before locking asymmetry. Write a small correspondence table for important asymmetric features: local side, expected screen side and visible/occluded state in each requested view. A face with an exposed mechanism cannot become a closed rear face merely by relabeling files. A user-requested whole-asset handedness correction is distinct from mirroring a side to invent an unseen view. State the front edge for top/bottom views. Hold configuration, camera, lighting, framing and background consistent. Unknown dimensions stay unknown; equal frame occupancy is not equal world size.

## Human or humanoid
Use requested A/T pose. If unspecified, propose A-pose with relaxed straight arms 30-45 degrees below horizontal, separated feet, neutral wrists and open hands. Keep it identical across front, anatomical left, back, anatomical right. Preserve face, age, hair, body ratios, height relationships, clothing, asymmetry and extreme stylization. Remove held items only as requested for modular separation.
Lock palm/dorsal surface direction and thumb side from the master, not from a generic pose name. For example, glove backs facing body-forward imply palms in the rear and narrow outer-hand edges in side elevations; visible knuckle armor should not repeat on all sides. Carry asymmetric pouch/strap banks by anatomical side, including which portions become occluded. Side projections foreshorten lateral arms and overlap fingers. Do not rotate wrists to display every finger; add a detail sheet when needed. Overlap is not proof of fusion or clearance.

## Creatures, animals, dinosaurs and monsters
Use an anatomically appropriate neutral stance for locomotion and limb count. Preserve joints, head/body ratios, wings, tails, horns, fins, claws, scales/fur and markings. Quadrupeds require a stable stance; bipeds need not have human arms. For flying creatures, choose one readable perched/resting or neutral airborne pose with fixed wing spread/fold across all directions. Optional spread-wing detail follows the required roster views. For unusual species, explicitly count locomotor limbs, manipulation limbs, wings and appendages separately; preserve attachment points and do not infer human anatomy from symmetry. Define axes for unusual anatomy. Default front/left/back/right; add top or appendage details when essential. Never force human A/T poses.

## Weapons and equipment
Define functional forward (muzzle/tool head), rear (stock/handle) and normal up. Preserve assembly state, moving/detachable components, openings, dimensions/proportions, materials and asymmetry. This is a visual asset reference.
Default front/local-left/back/local-right, with top when essential. Honor a requested side/front/rear/top set instead if specified. A side master such as master_left.png may best establish identity. Detail/separated-part sheets supplement the fixed assembled set, not replace requested views.

## Vehicles
Use front, local left, rear (back.png by default), local right elevations; optional top/underside. Fix wheel steering, ride height, suspension, doors/hatches/wings. Preserve wheelbase, track, axles/wheel count, cabin/body proportions, seams, lights, equipment, construction and asymmetry. Hidden underbody features are provisional.

## Buildings and architecture
Define main facade and local axes. Front/left/rear/right elevations share ground and roof datum. Preserve storeys, footprint/proportions, window/door counts and spacing, rooflines, chimneys, stairs, balconies, trim and repeated modules. Do not redesign the rear for composition. Add roof/top or structural views when essential/in scope. Unknown interiors/structure remain provisional; generated elevations are not measured engineering drawings.

## Props, furniture, machinery and environmental objects
Define front from function/construction (control face, seat front, access panel, emitting face). Four directional views by default. Fix assembled/resting/operating state and preserve part counts, proportions, seams, handles, feet, hinges, panels and material placement. Assign each asymmetric component to a specific local face/edge and carry its visibility through the set; do not duplicate an observed rivet row or control panel on both sides just to make each image look complete. No humanoid pose instructions or supporting hands. Top/bottom/detail/exploded views supplement assembled references when appropriate; preserve requested four.
